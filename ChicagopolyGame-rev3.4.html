Syndicate Dossier & Operation Rules

1. Underworld Objective & Quick Play

Bankrupt every rival mob family and seize complete authority over Prohibition-era Chicago. Note: All street tributes and penalties have been doubled to accelerate mob bankruptcies and shorten game length.

2. Acquirable Rackets vs. Municipal Squares

Why can only some squares be bought or auctioned?

• Acquirable Rackets (28 Squares): The 22 color-grouped illegal businesses, 4 Syndicate Limousines, and 2 Munitions Rings are private criminal operations[cite: 4]. When landed on while unowned, the active boss can seize them for the listed price or send them to open syndicate auction[cite: 4].

• Municipal / Event Squares (12 Squares): Squares such as The Big Payoff ($200 circuit reward), Risk, Petty Cash, Police Payoff / Tax Audits, Safe Haven, and Cook County Jail represent public city infrastructure or unexpected underworld events[cite: 4]. They cannot be purchased or owned by any boss[cite: 4].

3. Doubled Protection Tributes & Enforcers

• Stationing Hit Men: Once you own all rackets of a single color group, you can station up to 4 Hit Men per racket to drastically hike the tribute demanded from rivals[cite: 4].

• Promoting to Made Men: Stationing a 5th tier of muscle promotes the racket to an untouchable Made Man, yielding the maximum possible extortion payout[cite: 4].

• Syndicate Limousines (4): Tribute scales with each additional car owned ($50, $100, $200, $400)[cite: 4].

• Munitions Rings (2): Tribute equals 8x the dice roll if 1 is owned, or 20x the dice roll if both arms dealers are controlled[cite: 4].

4. Cook County Jail & Pardon Cards

If locked in Cook County Jail (via 3 consecutive doubles, landing on Federal Raid, or drawing an arrest warrant), you have 3 ways to get out on your turn:

1. Use a Jail Release Pardon Card: Expends your card and immediately frees you to roll and move[cite: 4].

2. Pay $50 Lawyer Bail: Pay a $50 fine before rolling[cite: 4].

3. Roll for Doubles: Attempt to roll doubles[cite: 4]. If successful, you move immediately without penalty[cite: 4]. If you fail 3 times, you must pay $50 and advance[cite: 4].

5. Saving & Resuming Games

Click "Save War" in the sidebar at any point[cite: 4]. Your entire game (cash, rackets, enforcers, turn order) is saved in your browser and can be resumed at any time using "Resume War"[cite: 4].
