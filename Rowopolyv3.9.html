River Guide & Regatta Rules

1. Charles River Objective & Quick Play

Bankrupt every rival crew captain and control the iconic boathouses along the Charles between Boston and Cambridge[cite: 2]. Note: All dock usage fees and penalties are doubled to accelerate crew eliminations and shorten game length.

2. Acquirable Boathouses vs. River Squares

Why can only some squares be bought or auctioned?

• Acquirable Boathouse Bays & Shells (28 Squares): The 22 color-grouped privileges across the river's historic clubs (Community Boating, Community Rowing Inc., Riverside Boat Club, Cambridge Boat Club, BU DeWolfe, MIT, Weld, Newell, and Union Boat Club)[cite: 2], 4 Racing Shells[cite: 2], and 2 Training Facilities. When landed on while unowned, the active captain can secure them for the listed price or send them to open regatta auction.

• River Landmark / Event Squares (12 Squares): Squares such as The Start Line ($200 circuit reward), River Hazards, Boathouse Locker, Basin Mooring Fee / USRowing Dues, Weeks Bridge Rest, and Dry Dock represent public waterways, course bridges, or navigation events[cite: 2]. They cannot be purchased or owned by any crew.

• Private Bidding (networked regatta): when a bay goes to open regatta auction, each captain bids or passes from their own screen with their own funds. Rival bid controls are shown dimmed and out of reach — nobody can spend another crew's money.

3. Doubled Dock Fees & Shell Rigging

• Rigging Racing Shells: Once you own all privileges of a single boathouse color group, you can rig up to 4 Racing Shells per bay to substantially increase the dock fees charged to rival crews.

• Championship Eight Shell: Adding a 5th tier of equipment promotes the bay to a full Championship Eight, commanding maximum usage fees.

• Racing Shells (4): Fees scale with each additional boat class controlled ($50, $100, $200, $400).

• Rowing Facilities (2): Usage fee equals 8x the dice roll if 1 facility is owned, or 20x the dice roll if both Ergometer Lab and Rigging Shop are controlled.

4. Dry Dock & Clearance Cards

If swamped or placed in Dry Dock (via 3 consecutive doubles, landing on Swamped!, or drawing a navigation penalty), you have 3 ways to return to the course on your turn:

1. Use a Dry Dock Clearance Card: Expends your card and immediately frees you to roll and stroke.

2. Pay $50 Harbor Bail: Pay a $50 fine before rolling.

3. Roll for Doubles: Attempt to roll doubles. If successful, you row out immediately without penalty. If you fail 3 times, you must pay $50 and advance.

5. Saving & Resuming Regattas

Click "Save Race" in the sidebar at any point. Your entire regatta (funds, boathouses, shells rigged, turn order) is saved in your browser and can be resumed at any time using "Resume Race".
